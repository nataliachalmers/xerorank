XEROrank Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026 · Applies to the XEROrank app for iPhone and iPad and the XEROrank web app

The short version

XEROrank does not collect, store or share personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only data that leaves your device is the medication names you enter, which are sent to two U.S. Government public databases to retrieve drug-label information and are not kept by us.

Who we are

XEROrank is published by Natalia Chalmers, DDS, MHSc, PhD (the "developer", "we"). Contact: natalia.chalmers@gmail.com.

What the app is for

XEROrank is a clinical decision support reference tool for licensed healthcare professionals. It scores medications for xerogenic (dry-mouth) potential from their current FDA labeling. It is not intended for patients to make medical decisions on their own.

Information you enter

The app asks for medication names only. Please do not enter patient names, dates of birth, record numbers or any other identifying information; the app has no field for them and no need for them. The medication list you enter is processed on your device to produce the scores, tier and draft note.

Information sent off your device

To look up a medication, the app sends the medication name (and nothing else about you or a patient) to:

• RxNav, operated by the U.S. National Library of Medicine (rxnav.nlm.nih.gov), to resolve the name to a standard RxNorm identifier; and
• openFDA, operated by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (api.fda.gov), to retrieve the current prescribing information.

These requests are made in real time, over encrypted connections, only to answer your query. We do not operate a server, do not receive these requests, and do not retain them. Like any web service, the two agencies may log technical details of requests (such as an IP address) under their own policies: NLM web policies and FDA website policies.

Information stored on your device

XEROrank keeps no patient records. It may keep small technical settings on your device (for example, your last-used display preferences) that never leave the device. Deleting the app removes them.

Chart notes you copy

When you copy a draft note into your practice-management or electronic health record system, that copy is governed by that system's and your organization's policies, not by this one.

Children

The app is intended for healthcare professionals and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you believe we have received personal information from you in error, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with data, we will update this page, change the effective date above, and describe the change in the app's release notes.
