XEROrank

Screen a patient's medication list for medication-induced dry mouth (xerostomia) risk, from the current FDA label — in seconds.

For healthcare professionals. XEROrank is a clinical decision support reference tool for dentists, dental hygienists, physicians, nurse practitioners, physician assistants and pharmacists. It does not diagnose, and any decision to start, stop or change a medication rests with the treating clinician.

How it works

Enter the medications. XEROrank resolves each name through the National Library of Medicine's RxNav service, retrieves the current FDA label from openFDA, and assigns a xerogenic-potential score from 0 to 3 using the XEROrank classification framework. Scores roll up into a regimen tier — low, moderate or high — and the app drafts a chart note you can copy into your record system.

XEROrank is an independent product: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration or the National Library of Medicine.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising. Medication names are sent to two U.S. Government public APIs to retrieve label data and are not stored. Read the privacy policy.
